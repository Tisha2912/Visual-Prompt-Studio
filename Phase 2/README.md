# The Last Voyage

## Medium A: Oil Painting

### Prompt A
Okay, lets create an oil painting with a dramatic effect portraying a large wooden ship sailing in a violent storm at sea as it crashes into uneven rocks during the night time.The lines of towering waves and the lightning need to be emphasized, along with the rough ocean texture.Other textures to be emphasized include , weathered wood and dark storm clouds. Also create a contrast between the dark depp shadows and the bright lightning, and also the sense of movement created by the powerful wind and the waves that are crashing.Use oil paints, fine and broader paintbrushes, and a traditional layering, glazing environment or atmosphere. The composition's main focus should be on the ship as the central point of emphasis, along with dramatic lighting reflecting across the water and a unified, realistic scene that captures the chaos, danger, and emotion of the storm

## Medium B: Woodblock Print

### Prompt B
Now lets create a woodblock print with a dramatic effect potraying a large wooden ship sailing in a violent storm at sea as it crashes into uneven rocks during the night time.The lines of towering waves and the lightning need to be emphasized, also the texture of the carved wood surface, the strong contrast between black and white, and the sense of movement created by the crashing waves and powerful wind. Use traditional woodblock carving tools, wooden printing blocks, and relief printing techniques with bold carved lines, simplified shapes, and expressive textures to create the image. The composition should keep the ship as the central point of emphasis, with dramatic carved patterns representing the storm, creating a unified and visually powerful print that captures the chaos, danger, and emotion of the ship's final journey or voyage.
