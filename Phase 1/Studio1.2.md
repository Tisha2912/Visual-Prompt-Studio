# Neon Horizon

## Genre Choice

Synthwave / Retro 1980s

## Copilot Prompt

Okay, so I want you to create a high-quality digital illustration for a retro 1980s synthwave album cover. Title it Neon Horizon. The scene features a sleek, chrome sports car that is parked on the neon grid highway, that's stretches into a futuristic cityline at sunset. There should be tall skyscraper and skyscrapers with looming windows rise in the background. And there should be palm trees lined on both side of the road. Use a vibrant color palette of pink, purple, orange, cyan, and blue with coral to create the retro aesthetics. Place a brilliant glowing setting down sun low on the horizon, which would cast a warm light across the scene, creating shadows, creating the shadows that would be surrounding the building, car, and create a contrast or like a dramatic mood. Also show depth by making roads, palm trees, and buildings gradually smaller and fading into the distance, which would create a space illusion. Motion should be there by showing passage of time with glowing light trails, clouds, reflections on the road. Also create a combined geometric laser grid with the palm trees and reflected chrome, mesh, silver surfaces while maintaining unity throughout a consistent neon color palette. Use some asymmetrical balance by placing the sports car on the left while allowing the tower skyline to dominate the opposite right side. Make the skyscrapers much larger than the car to emphasize the scale and also like create a pop focal point. Leave the space at the top for glowing album title Neon Horizon in bold, retro-ish fonts.

## Vocabulary Breakdown

### Value

Value is the intensity of the light and dark parts of an artwork are. It would create contrasts and alos set the entire design together.

### Space

The depth and distance in an artwork used with other elemnts like size, fading  to give the 3-D effect can be defined as space.

### Unity

Unity of an artwork is when all elements the shapes, colors, design come together and feel compleye.

### Balance

The overall stablility of the artwork irrespective of unevenly placed elemnts or objects can be termed as Balance.

### Rhythm

Rythm is created by the repetition in colors, lines, shapes, or patterns throughout an artwork.
